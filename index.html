A Poem

"The Girl Who Chose Her World"

Born beneath a Halloween sky,
while the world dressed up in disguise,
you arrived, no costume, no mask,
just you, in your own quiet grace.
~ ~ ~
She reads in the language of pages,
she writes in worlds only she knows,
she watches monsters for comfort,
while other hearts run from the shadows.
~ ~ ~
She's not lost, she's just elsewhere,
in a world too real for crowded noise,
her peace is a fortress she built herself,
and you can't break what she chose to enjoy.
~ ~ ~
Here's to the girl who's nonchalant and sweet,
who loves her dogs and her old OPM,
who eats her fries and reacts to messages,
and somehow makes it all look effortless.
~ ~ ~
Happy Birthday, Andrea.
Stay exactly who you are,
you are the kind of rare
the world doesn't quite understand yet.
